# Community code of conduct

Foundry Relay welcomes constructive participation from people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful. Critique ideas and code rather than people. No harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, sexual harassment, or spam. Respect privacy and avoid posting personal information without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation to protect the community. Report concerns through GitHub's available moderation/reporting mechanisms; avoid public escalation of sensitive complaints.

This is a provisional project policy, subject to maintainer review before active development.
